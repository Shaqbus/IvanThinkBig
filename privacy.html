Privacy Policy — Veeqo Packaging Marketplace (Chrome Extension)

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Veeqo Packaging Marketplace — Overlay Chrome extension ("the extension") handles information. The extension adds a "Packaging Marketplace" button to the Veeqo web app and shows a packaging-reorder estimate in its popup.

Summary

The extension does not collect, sell, or transmit your personal information. It stores a few configuration settings locally in your browser and does not track your browsing. The only information that leaves your browser is a link-out attribution token — described below — and only when you click to open the storefront.

What the extension stores

The extension uses Chrome's storage.sync to save a small set of settings so they persist between sessions and across your signed-in Chrome profile:

  • Storefront URL — the marketplace address the button links to.
  • Seller identifier — a label you set, used to build the link-out attribution token.
  • Reorder display preferences — a simulated shipment-volume number and a period-in-days value that drive the on-hand and days-until-out estimates shown in the popup.

That is the complete list. No browsing history, page content, credentials, payment data, or personal information is stored.

What the extension accesses on web pages

The extension runs a content script on veeqo.com domains (https://app.veeqo.com/* and https://*.veeqo.com/*) for the sole purpose of placing its button in the Veeqo header. It reads only the page's navigation structure to position the button correctly. It does not read, collect, or transmit page content, form inputs, order data, or any personal information from Veeqo. It runs on no other websites.

Information that leaves your browser

When you click the "Packaging Marketplace" button, the extension opens the storefront in a new tab with an attribution token in the URL. This token is generated locally and consists of your configured seller identifier, a timestamp, and a random value. Its only purpose is to attribute the visit to Veeqo. It contains no data scraped from the Veeqo page and no personal information beyond the seller identifier you chose to set.

How information is used

  • Stored settings are used only to operate the extension (link to the right storefront and show reorder estimates).
  • The attribution token is used only for link-out attribution to the storefront you open.
  • We do not sell data to third parties.
  • We do not use data for any purpose unrelated to the extension's single purpose.
  • We do not use data to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Data sharing and third parties

The extension does not send data to any analytics service, advertising network, or third-party server. Settings stay in your browser via Chrome storage. Opening the storefront navigates your browser to that site, which is governed by its own terms.

Data retention and removal

Settings persist in Chrome storage until you change them or remove the extension. Uninstalling the extension removes its stored settings.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's data practices change (for example, if a future version reads shipment data directly from Veeqo), this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the Veeqo Packaging Marketplace team via the listing's developer contact on the Chrome Web Store.
